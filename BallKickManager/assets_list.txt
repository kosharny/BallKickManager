# Asset Documentation
List of image assets required for the application. Add these to your Xcode Assets.xcassets catalog with the exact names listed below.

## 1. Onboarding Images
These images are displayed during the initial user setup.
- **`onboarding_leg`**:
  - *Context*: displayed on the "Dominant Leg" selection screen.
  - *Description*: An illustration or photo of a football player kicking a ball or highlighting leg strength.
  
- **`onboarding_stats`**:
  - *Context*: displayed on the "Body Stats" screen (Height/Weight).
  - *Description*: An icon or illustration representing measurement, a scale, or athletic physique.
  
- **`onboarding_goal`**:
  - *Context*: displayed on the "Main Goal" selection screen.
  - *Description*: An image of a target, a goal net, or a trophy representing achievement.

## 2. Content Images (Articles & Tasks)
These images are used for Card headers and Detail views.
- **`kick_ball_action`**:
  - *Used In*: Article A1 (Knuckleball), Task T9 (Midfielder's Vision).
  - *Description*: Dynamic shot of a player striking the ball.
  
- **`dribbling_drill`**:
  - *Used In*: Task T1 (Dribbling), Task T6 (First Touch), Task T8 (Wall Ball).
  - *Description*: Close-up of ball at feet or player navigating cones.
  
- **`tactical_setup`**:
  - *Used In*: Article A8 (Through Ball), Article A11 (Gegenpressing).
  - *Description*: A tactical view of a pitch, formation board, or coach pointing.
  
- **`soccer_strategy_board`**:
  - *Used In*: Article A9 (Offside Trap).
  - *Description*: A whiteboard with Xs and Os or player movement arrows.
  
- **`player_stretching`**:
  - *Used In*: Article A7 (Active Recovery).
  - *Description*: Player doing stretches or foam rolling.
  
- **`training_field_night`**:
  - *Used In*: Article A10 (Captaincy), Task T7 (Defender's School).
  - *Description*: A moody, lit stadium or training ground at night.
  
- **`penalty_spot`**:
  - *Used In*: Task T10 (Goalkeeper Basics).
  - *Description*: View from the penalty spot or goalkeeper's perspective.

## 3. General
- **`app_icon`**: The main application icon.
